# BML Munjal University – Semester Examination Policy & Guidelines

## 1. Overview and Applicability
This policy applies to all undergraduate (B.Tech, BCA, B.Sc, BBA) and postgraduate (M.Tech, MCA, MBA) programs conducted at BML Munjal University. The examination framework operates under the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) governed by the Office of the Controller of Examinations (CoE).

---

## 2. Examination Structure & Credit Weightage
Every regular semester course is evaluated using Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) and an End-Semester Examination (ESE).

| Assessment Component | Weightage | Minimum Passing % | Remarks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA)** | 40% | 40% (16/40) | Quizzes, Mid-Term Exams, Assignments, Lab Work |
| **End-Semester Examination (ESE)** | 60% | 40% (24/60) | Comprehensive 3-hour theoretical/practical paper |
| **Overall Aggregate** | 100% | 40% (40/100) | Mandatory combined total to be declared PASS |

> **Note on Laboratory Courses:** For practical/laboratory courses, the CIA accounts for 50% and the ESE accounts for 50%. A student must secure at least 50% separately in practical ESE to clear the course.

---

## 3. Admit Card & Examination Hall Regulations
1. **Admit Card Issuance:** Hall tickets / Admit Cards are released through the student ERP portal 7 days prior to the commencement of ESE.
2. **Eligibility Criteria for Admit Card:**
   - Minimum 75% aggregate course attendance (or valid approved condonation).
   - "No Dues" clearance from the Finance Department, Hostel Administration, and Central Library.
3. **Entry Rules:**
   - Students must enter the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time.
   - Entry is strictly prohibited 30 minutes after the exam has commenced.
   - No student is permitted to leave the examination hall before 90 minutes of examination time have elapsed.
4. **Prohibited Items:** Mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, electronic memory devices, and loose notes. Only standard non-programmable scientific calculators (Casio fx-82/fx-991 series) are permissible where explicitly authorized on the question paper.

---

## 4. Backlog Examinations & Supplementary Attempts
1. **Definition of Backlog:** A course is recorded as a backlog if a student receives an 'F' (Fail) or 'Ab' (Absent) grade.
2. **Remedial Examination Windows:**
   - **Odd Semester Backlogs:** Conducted during the next regular Odd Semester examination cycle (November–December).
   - **Even Semester Backlogs:** Conducted during the next regular Even Semester examination cycle (April–May).
   - **Summer Supplementary Term:** Held in June–July exclusively for final-year graduating students with a maximum of 3 pending arrears.
3. **Examination Arrear Fees:**
   - Regular backlog registration fee: **₹750 per subject**.
   - Summer supplementary fee: **₹1,500 per subject**.
   - Maximum allowable backlog registrations in a regular semester: **4 subjects** alongside regular semester load.

---

## 5. Re-Evaluation and Answer Script Verification
Students who are not satisfied with their ESE results can request answer script verification and re-evaluation within **14 calendar days** from the date of result declaration on the portal.

1. **Step 1: Soft Copy of Evaluated Script (Photocopy Verification)**
   - Fee: **₹300 per subject**.
   - Student receives an evaluated digital copy with marking rubric within 5 working days.
2. **Step 2: Formal Re-Evaluation**
   - Fee: **₹800 per subject**.
   - Script is assigned to an independent external examiner.
   - If the revised mark varies by more than **15%**, the script is sent to a third evaluator, and the average of the two nearest marks is finalized.
   - If marks increase by **10% or more**, a 50% fee refund (₹400) is credited back to the student's institutional account.

---

## 6. Unfair Means Committee (UMC) & Penalties
The University maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding academic malpractice:
- **Category 1 (Possession of unauthorized material without use):** Cancellation of the current paper and award of 'F' grade for that course.
- **Category 2 (Copying from notes, devices, or another student):** Cancellation of all registered courses in the current semester and debared from attending one subsequent semester.
- **Category 3 (Impersonation / Tampering with answer sheets):** Immediate expulsion from the University and registration of a formal police complaint under state university examination act.
- Appeals against UMC rulings must be submitted in writing to the Registrar within 7 days of the verdict.
