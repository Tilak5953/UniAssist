# BML Munjal University – Student Attendance Policy & Condonation Rules

## 1. Statutory Attendance Requirement
Regular academic attendance is considered an indispensable component of the learning experience at BML Munjal University.
1. Every registered student is expected to maintain a minimum of **75% aggregate attendance** in every individual course (theory lectures, tutorials, and practical lab sessions separately).
2. Students falling below 75% attendance are automatically flagged on the Academic ERP as **Debarred (Ineligible)** from appearing in the End-Semester Examination for that specific course.

---

## 2. Categories of Condonation (Relaxation)
Under exceptional and documented circumstances, the University allows a maximum attendance relaxation (condonation) of up to **10%**, thereby bringing the absolute minimum cutoff to **65%**.

| Attendance Bracket | Eligibility Status | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **75% – 100%** | Full Eligibility | Automatically eligible for End-Semester Examinations (Admit Card granted) |
| **65% – 74.9%** | Condonation Eligible | Condonation approval required from Dean of Academic Affairs with ₹1,200 fine |
| **Below 65%** | Strictly Debarred (Year / Course Repeat) | No condonation granted under any circumstance. Must re-register for the course |

---

## 3. Medical Leave Protocol & Documentation
In cases of serious illness, hospitalization, or surgical procedures:
1. The student or guardian must submit a medical leave application accompanied by:
   - Legible medical practitioner certificate with registration number.
   - Hospital admission and discharge summary (if hospitalized).
   - Medical prescriptions, diagnostic test reports, and pharmacy receipts.
2. **Submission Deadline:** The documentation must be submitted to the respective Head of Department (HoD) within **7 working days** of returning to campus. Late submissions beyond 15 days will be summarily rejected.
3. Medical leave cannot be claimed retroactively at the end of the semester after the debarment list is published.

---

## 4. Official Duty Leave (OD / On-Duty Attendance)
Students representing the University in approved extracurricular, sports, hackathons, or academic events are entitled to Official Duty (OD) credits:
- **Eligible Events:** Inter-University sports competitions, State/National level hackathons, paper presentations at IEEE/ACM conferences, and official University student council responsibilities.
- **Quota Limit:** A maximum of **10 calendar days per semester** may be claimed as On-Duty leave.
- **Approval Workflow:** 
  1. Advance recommendation from the Faculty Advisor / Sports Director before leaving campus.
  2. Submission of participation or achievement certificate within 5 working days post-event to the Academic Office.
  3. Approved ODs are counted as 100% present in the attendance calculation.

---

## 5. Attendance Debarment Notice & Appeals Mechanism
1. **Warning Notifications:**
   - **First Warning Notice:** Sent via SMS and student email when attendance drops below 80% at the end of the 6th teaching week.
   - **Final Debarment Notice:** Published by the Dean's Office 10 days before the start of final exams.
2. **Right to Appeal:**
   - A debarred student may file an appeal before the **Attendance Redressal Committee** within 48 hours of notice publication.
   - The committee conducts hearings on day 3 post-notice and delivers a final binding decision.
3. **Consequences of Debarment:**
   - The student receives an 'FA' (Failed due to Attendance) grade on the transcript.
   - The student must re-register for the course in the upcoming summer semester or whenever the course is next offered by paying regular course re-registration fees (₹3,500 per credit).
