# BML Munjal University – Academic Regulations & Grading System

## 1. Credit Structure & Graduation Requirements
1. **Curriculum Framework:** The University adopts the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS).
2. **Total Credits Required for Degree Completion:**
   - **B.Tech (4-Year Program):** Minimum 160 earned credits.
   - **B.Tech (Lateral Entry 3-Year):** Minimum 120 earned credits.
   - **BCA / B.Sc / BBA (3-Year Program):** Minimum 120 earned credits.
   - **M.Tech / MCA / MBA (2-Year Program):** Minimum 80 earned credits.
3. **Credit Definition:** 
   - 1 Lecture hour per week = 1 Credit.
   - 1 Tutorial hour per week = 1 Credit.
   - 2 Practical/Laboratory hours per week = 1 Credit.

## 2. 10-Point Letter Grading Scale
Academic performance in every course is awarded as a letter grade corresponding to grade points as follows:

| Letter Grade | Performance Description | Grade Points | Percentage Equivalent Range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **O** | Outstanding | 10 | 90% and above |
| **A+** | Excellent | 9 | 80% to 89% |
| **A** | Very Good | 8 | 70% to 79% |
| **B+** | Good | 7 | 60% to 69% |
| **B** | Above Average | 6 | 55% to 59% |
| **C** | Average | 5 | 50% to 54% |
| **P** | Pass | 4 | 40% to 49% |
| **F** | Fail / Re-appear | 0 | Less than 40% |
| **Ab** | Absent | 0 | Did not appear for End-Sem Exam |
| **FA** | Debarred for Attendance | 0 | Attendance below required threshold |

## 3. Computation of SGPA and CGPA
1. **Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA):**
   Calculated at the end of each semester as:
   $$\text{SGPA} = \frac{\sum (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum C_i}$$
   Where $C_i$ is the credit of the $i$-th course and $G_i$ is the grade point earned.
2. **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):**
   Reflects cumulative performance across all completed semesters:
   $$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\sum (C_{\text{all}} \times G_{\text{all}})}{\sum C_{\text{all}}}$$
3. **Conversion to Equivalent Percentage:**
   $$\text{Equivalent Percentage} = (\text{CGPA} - 0.75) \times 10$$

---

## 4. Academic Probation and Detention Rules
1. **Academic Warning:** Issued if a student's SGPA falls below 5.50 in any single semester.
2. **Academic Probation:**
   - A student whose CGPA drops below **5.00** is placed on Academic Probation for the subsequent semester.
   - A student on probation is barred from holding executive student council posts and cannot register for course overloads.
3. **Year-Back / Detention Criterion:**
   - Promotion to 2nd Year (Semester 3): Student must have earned at least 50% of the total 1st year credits (minimum 20 out of 40 credits).
   - Promotion to 3rd Year (Semester 5): Student must have cleared all courses of 1st year without any active backlog, plus 50% of 2nd year credits.
   - Promotion to 4th Year (Semester 7): Student must have cleared all courses of 1st and 2nd years completely.

---

## 5. Branch Transfer (Change of Engineering Discipline)
Branch transfer at the end of the first academic year is permissible subject to strict merit:
1. **Eligibility Criteria:**
   - Must have cleared all Semester 1 and Semester 2 courses in the **first attempt** with no backlogs or grade revisions.
   - Minimum CGPA of **8.50** at the end of Semester 2.
2. **Quota Constraint:** Total intake in any recipient branch cannot exceed 10% of sanctioned strength.
3. **Application Deadline:** Submitted to the Academic Dean within 10 days of the announcement of 2nd semester results.
