## 2. Payment Timelines & Late Fine Schedule
1. **Due Dates:**
   - **Odd Semester:** July 31st of each academic year.
   - **Even Semester:** January 15th of each academic year.
2. **Late Fine Structure:**
   - Days 1 to 10 past due date: **₹100 per day**.
   - Days 11 to 20 past due date: **₹250 per day**.
   - After Day 20: ERP access and course registration are temporarily frozen until full settlement plus a flat administrative reinstatement fine of **₹5,000**.
3. **Payment Modes:** Only online payments via Net Banking, UPI, and Debit/Credit Cards through the university portal are recognized. Cash deposits in university accounts are strictly disallowed.

---

## 3. Merit & Need-Based Scholarships
BML Munjal University awards over ₹5 Crores annually in financial aid across various categories:

### A. Academic Excellence Merit Scholarship
- **Category I (Top 2% of Batch / CGPA >= 9.50):** 75% tuition fee waiver for the following academic year.
- **Category II (Top 5% of Batch / CGPA >= 9.00 to 9.49):** 50% tuition fee waiver for the following academic year.
- **Category III (Top 10% of Batch / CGPA >= 8.50 to 8.99):** 25% tuition fee waiver for the following academic year.
- *Condition:* Recipient must not have any active backlogs and must have 80%+ attendance.

### B. Sports & Extra-Curricular Scholarship
- International medalists/participants: 100% full tuition & hostel waiver.
- National level medalists: 50% tuition fee waiver.
- State level winners: 25% tuition fee waiver.

### C. Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (Need-Based)
- Available for students with family annual gross income below ₹3.5 Lakhs.
- Awards 30% to 50% tuition concessions on annual review by the University Welfare Board.

## 4. Fee Refund Policy (Withdrawal from Program)
In alignment with statutory UGC regulations, refunds for students withdrawing from a program are structured as follows:

| Withdrawal Request Timing | Percentage Refund of Aggregate Fees | Deductions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **15 days or more before formally notified last date of admission** | 100% | Maximum ₹1,000 processing fee |
| **Less than 15 days before formally notified last date of admission** | 90% | 10% administrative deduction |
| **15 days or less after formally notified last date of admission** | 80% | 20% administrative deduction |
| **More than 15 days but less than 30 days after last date of admission** | 50% | 50% deduction |
| **More than 30 days after formally notified last date of admission** | 0% | Only refundable caution deposit returned |
