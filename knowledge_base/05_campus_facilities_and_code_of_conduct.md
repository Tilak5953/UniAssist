# Apex Global University – Campus Facilities & Code of Student Conduct

## 1. Central Library Regulations & Services
1. **Operating Hours:**
   - Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
   - Sunday & Public Holidays: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
   - During End-Semester Examinations: Open 24/7 with round-the-clock security and Wi-Fi access.
2. **Borrowing Limits & Loan Period:**
   - Undergraduate Students: Up to **4 books** for a duration of **14 days**.
   - Postgraduate Students: Up to **6 books** for a duration of **21 days**.
3. **Late Overdue Fines:**
   - Days 1 to 7 overdue: **₹5 per book per day**.
   - Days 8 to 15 overdue: **₹10 per book per day**.
   - After 15 days: Library card is suspended, and replacement cost of the book plus 20% surcharge is charged.
4. **Digital Library Access:** All registered students are provided free remote credentials for IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, ACM Digital Library, and Springer journals via the University MyLOFT portal.

---

## 2. Residence Halls & Hostel Regulations
1. **Hostel Timings & Curfew:**
   - General Campus Gate Closing: **10:00 PM**.
   - Hostel Biometric In-Time: **10:30 PM**.
   - Students arriving between 10:30 PM and 11:15 PM will receive a Late Entry Warning. Accumulation of 3 late entries in a semester results in parent notification and ₹1,000 fine.
2. **Night-Out / Leave Protocol:**
   - Any student planning to stay out overnight must submit an e-gate pass request through the Hostel ERP at least **12 hours in advance**.
   - Mandatory automated SMS confirmation is sent to and approved by registered parents/guardians before the security gate allows exit.
3. **Prohibited Conduct in Hostels:**
   - Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, narcotics, or e-cigarettes is strictly prohibited (Immediate suspension and ₹20,000 penalty).
   - High-wattage electronic appliances (immersion heaters, electric stoves, induction cookers, heavy heaters) are banned for fire safety reasons.

---

## 3. Strict Anti-Ragging Policy & Helplines
In adherence to Supreme Court guidelines and UGC regulations, Apex Global University follows a **Zero Tolerance Policy** toward ragging in any form:
- **Definition:** Any disorderly conduct, spoken or written, teasing, treating, handling with rudeness, or causing psychological harm or fear.
- **Punitive Actions:** Immediate suspension, expulsion from hostel and academic institution, debarment from campus placements, and filing of a First Information Report (FIR) under the Indian Penal Code.
- **Emergency Helplines:**
  - Campus Anti-Ragging Flying Squad (24/7): **+91-11-2800-4499**
  - National Anti-Ragging Helpline (Toll-Free): **1800-180-5522**
  - Confidential Email: **antiragging@apexuniv.ac.in**

## 4. Health Centre & Medical Emergencies
1. The University operates a 24/7 Primary Health Care Centre with resident medical officers, 4 observation beds, and an on-campus Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) ambulance.
2. In case of medical emergencies inside dormitories, students should dial the Campus Emergency Dispatch at **Ext. 108 / 112**.
3. Basic consultation, first aid, OTC medications, and basic diagnostic tests are free of charge for all enrolled students.

---

## 5. Student Grievance Redressal Mechanism
Students experiencing administrative, academic, or accommodation difficulties can access the University Ombudsman and Grievance Committee:
1. **Step 1:** Register ticket on the online Grievance Portal (`https://grievance.apexuniv.ac.in`).
2. **Step 2:** Resolution timeline is guaranteed within **7 working days** by the designated committee.
3. **Step 3:** Unresolved grievances may be escalated directly to the University Ombudsman.
